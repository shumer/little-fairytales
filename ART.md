# Графика

Фон `public/garden.png` создан встроенным инструментом ImageGen. Исходник скопирован в проект. Игровые слои реализованы как векторные изображения в `src/art.ts`, чтобы детали одежды точно совпадали с фиксированной позой героя.

## Промпт фона

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: background for a preschool 2D mobile dress-up game. Create a portrait 2:3 full-bleed soft storybook watercolor illustration of a magical garden clearing at sunrise. Warm ivory sky, blush pink fluffy clouds, very distant lavender hills, pale mint lawn. Delicate pink flowering trees frame left and right edges, tiny daisies along the bottom edge. Center 65 percent of the image must remain very light, quiet, empty and low-detail to place a large game character or castle over it. Sophisticated children's picture book, rounded friendly forms, subtle paper grain, soft hand-painted edges, pastel peach lavender sage palette. No people, no animals, no buildings, no castle, no typography, no text, no UI, no watermarks. Save output file for use in local game.
